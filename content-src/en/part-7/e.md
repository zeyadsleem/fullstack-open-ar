In addition to the six exercises in the <a href="https://courses.mooc.fi/org/uh-cs/courses/full-stack-open-extension/chapter-2" target="_blank" rel="noopener">React Hooks</a> sections of this part of the course material, 14 exercises continue our work on the BlogList application that we worked on in parts four and five of the course material.

If you do not want to use your BlogList application, you are free to use the code from the model solutions as a starting point for these exercises.

Many of the exercises in this part of the course material will require the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code_refactoring" target="_blank" rel="noopener">refactoring</a> of existing code. This is a common reality of extending existing applications, meaning that refactoring is an important and necessary skill, even if it may feel difficult and unpleasant at times.

One good piece of advice for both refactoring and writing new code is to take&nbsp;<em>baby steps</em>. Losing your sanity is almost guaranteed if you leave the application in a completely broken state for long periods while refactoring.

<strong>These exercises assume that you have already completed exercises <a href="https://fullstackopen.com//en/part5/react_router_ui_frameworks#exercises-5-24-5-28" target="_blank" rel="noopener">5.24-5.28</a>. If you have not, do those first.</strong>
